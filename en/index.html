Common questions

Are you a dealer?

No. We connect you with licensed Texas dealers. They sell the vehicles and, with their lenders, decide approval and terms.

I have bad credit. Can I still buy?

Many of our dealers work with bad credit and thin credit files. Approval is up to the dealer or lender, and we'll be upfront about what to expect.

I had a repo or a bankruptcy.

Some dealers work with those situations. Tell us what happened and we'll give you an honest answer about your options.

This is my first car. Where do I start?

Build your plan or message us. Many of our dealers work with first-time buyers and people with no credit history.

Will you pull my credit?

We won't. Building your plan doesn't touch your credit report. If you move forward with a dealer, they may check it with your permission.

How much do I need down?

It depends on the vehicle, your situation and the lender. That's why we don't advertise numbers: the dealer gives them to you in writing. We'll point you in the right direction on WhatsApp.

What documents do I need?

Usually an ID, proof of income and proof of address. Your dealer will tell you exactly what they need. We never ask for document numbers on this site.

I'm paid in cash or self-employed.

There are ways to show income, like bank statements or an employer letter. We'll walk you through it.

Are the vehicles used? Clean title?

Each listing shows the condition. Confirm the history and title status directly with the dealer before you decide.

Who sees my information?

Only the partner dealers you authorize. This page doesn't store your answers: we only see them if you send the message on WhatsApp. Read the privacy policy.
